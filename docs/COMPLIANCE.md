# Compliance architecture roadmap

The current standalone milestone supplies capability enforcement and audit infrastructure; it does not claim regulatory compliance or surveillance coverage.

Future compliance decisions will record rule identifier/version, input snapshot, user, result, explanations suitable for review, and timestamp. Rules will be separated into pre-trade, post-trade, communications, research, and data-access policies. Surveillance outputs will be analytical alerts requiring validated models and human review, not assertions of misconduct or regulatory certainty.
